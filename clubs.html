Clubs

Explore all of our different clubs and activities offered here at Rigby High School.

Academic & CTE Clubs

Discover career pathways, hands-on technical training, and competitive academic teams. These student organizations bridge classroom learning with real-world industry experience, state leadership conferences, and STEM challenges.

VEX Robotics (Team 9776):

  • Overview: Competitive STEM program where student teams design, build, and program custom robots to compete in the VEX V5 game challenge.
  • Activities: Autonomous programming, mechanism design, chassis building, driving practice, and competing at tournaments.
  • Meeting Time & Location: After school in the CTE Robotics Lab.

BPA (Business Professionals of America):

  • Overview: Student organization centered on developing leadership, business management, digital design, and financial software skills.
  • Activities: Regional, state, and national leadership conferences, competitive business events, and community service projects.
  • Meeting Time & Location: After school on Tuesdays and Thursdays in room 2610.

FFA (Future Farmers of America):

  • Overview: Premier youth organization preparing members for leadership and careers in agriculture, science, and natural resources.
  • Activities: Livestock evaluation, agriscience projects, leadership conventions, and local community service.
  • Meeting Time & Location: 6th period and after school in the Ag building.

SkillsUSA:

  • Overview: Partnership of students, teachers, and industry working together to ensure America has a skilled workforce.
  • Activities: Hands-on competitions in trades, technical drafting, automotive technology, and leadership.
  • Meeting Time & Location: Afterschool on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday in the Ag building.

Leadership & Community Service

Build essential leadership skills and make an impact on campus and across Eastern Idaho. These service driven organizations empower students to lead school events, volunteer with local nonprofits, and represent the Trojan student body.

Student Council (STUCO):

  • Overview: Student-elected leadership body representing the student body and organizing school-wide activities.
  • Activities: Homecoming week, dances, spirit weeks, and student advocacy.
  • Meeting Time & Location: Afterschool everyday in room 1842.

National Honor Society (NHS):

  • Overview: Organization honoring high school students who demonstrate excellence in scholarship, service, leadership, and character.
  • Activities: Peer tutoring, community service drives, campus cleanups, and leadership development.
  • Requirements: Minimum GPA threshold and invitation/application.
  • Meeting Time & Location: 4th period every school day, located in room 2403.

Rotary Club:

  • Overview: Student-led service organization encouraging leadership through serving others in the local community.
  • Activities: Food drives, volunteering with local non-profits, and fundraising events.
  • Meeting Time & Location: After school Thursdays and Fridays, some Saturday mornings in room 1612.

Fine Arts & Performance

Express your creativity through performance, music, and public speaking. Whether on stage, in the band, or at debate podiums, these programs showcase Trojan talent across regional competitions and school productions.

Drama & Theater Club:

  • Overview: Creative home for students interested in acting, stagecraft, costume design, and technical theater production.
  • Activities: Plays, musicals, performances, and set design workshops.
  • Meeting Time & Location: During 6th period and afterschool every day in room 1412.

Marching Band & Pep Band:

  • Overview: Musical performance groups supplying high-energy music at Trojan athletic events and regional band competitions.
  • Activities: Football games, basketball games, halftime shows, parades, plays, and band concerts.
  • Meeting Time & Location: During 1st period and afterschool when it is needed (Games and plays) in room 1414.

Speech & Debate:

  • Overview: Competitive academic team focusing on public speaking, policy debate, and rhetorical performance.
  • Activities: Interscholastic debate tournaments across Idaho, public showcase events, and state qualifiers.
  • Meeting Time & Location: 4th period during the school day in room 1618.

Special Interest & Hobbies

Connect with fellow students who share your passions outside the standard curriculum. From competitive video gaming and studio art to outdoor recreation across Idaho, these clubs offer great ways to get involved and try new things.

Esports Team:

  • Overview: Competitive video gaming team representing Rigby High School in sanctioned high school leagues (IHSAA / PlayVS).
  • Activities: Team matches in games like League of Legends, Rocket League, and Smash Bros, focusing on strategy and communication.
  • Meeting Time & Location: 5th period in room 2204.

Art Club:

  • Overview: Open studio space for artists of all skill levels to experiment with visual media, paint, and digital art.
  • Activities: Murals, art showcases, seasonal craft projects, and open studio sessions.
  • Meeting Time & Location: Everyday afterschool in room 1814.

Outdoors & Recreation Club:

  • Overview: Group dedicated to promoting outdoor sports, trail awareness, and environmental appreciation around Eastern Idaho.
  • Activities: Day hikes, mountain biking outings, outdoor safety workshops, and trail maintenance.
  • Meeting Time & Location: Afterschool on Monday's as well as the first and third saturday of the month.
