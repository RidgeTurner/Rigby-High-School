Team Rosters

Multi-Tier Teams:

Most Trojan sports have three complete roster levels (Freshman, Junior Varsity (JV), and Varsity) to support player development across all grade levels.

Roster Information:

The team rosters detail player jersey numbers, positions, height/weight profiles, graduation years, and captain designations.

Coaching Staff:

Rosters also have information about head coaches, assistant staff, and athletic trainers that lead each program, maintaining strict academic eligibility requirements (minimum GPA standards and credit completion) under IHSAA rules.

Where to find:

Team rosters can be found at: rigbyathletic.rankone.com/Website/Main

Team Schedules

6A High Country Conference (HCC):

Rigby High School competes in Idaho's 6A High Country Conference. Schedules reflect both intra-conference matchups and non-conference competitions across the region.

Game Day Timings & Locations:

Varsity football games take place on Friday nights at 7:00 PM at the Trojan stadium. Mid-week games for soccer, volleyball, and basketball typically start with the JV team at 5:30 Pm, followed by the Varsity team at 7:00 PM.

Seasonal Athletic Calendars:

Schedules are organized by season. The seasons that each sport is in can be found above.
