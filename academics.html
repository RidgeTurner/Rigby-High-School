Associate Degress & Certificate Options

Students at Rigby High School can complete college degrees while still in high school using Idaho's Advanced Opportunities funding. The different pathways can be found below:

Associate of Arts (A.A.) Degree:

  • Partner Institution: College of Southern Idaho (CSI).
  • Overview: A 60 to 64 credit degree track focused on general education, liberal arts, communication, and social sciences.
  • Timeline: Designed for students who start planning during their freshman/sophomore year to complete general education requirements and electives alongside high school graduation repuirements.

GEM / General Education Academic Certificate:

  • Overview: A 36 credit general education framework (General Education Matriculation) that covers fundamental core college categories: English, Mathematics, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Natural Sciences.
  • Transferability: Fulfills lower-division general education core requirements across all Idaho public universities (ISU, BSU, UI, LCSC).

Technical / Vocational Certificates:

  • Partner Institution: College of Eastern Idaho (CEI).
  • Overview: Career and technical tracks that build toward industry certifications or Associate of Applied Science (A.A.S.) credits in fields like mechanics, safety, and health professions.
